Business problem

Capacity decisions are faster than the reporting cycle.

Hospital leaders often reconcile bed board, ED, OR, staffing, and discharge context manually. The command center turns those fragmented signals into a shared, near-real-time operating picture.

Target audience

Clinical operations, nursing, ED, perioperative, and executive leadership.

The app supports huddle preparation, surge response, throughput monitoring, discharge coordination, and escalation management.

Platform value

One governed data path for analytics and operational action.

Databricks provides the foundation for streaming data, curated features, model-ready forecasting, and consistent governance across the command-center workflow.
