Platform data products

Separate the platform products from the analytics experiences.

These are the reusable data products and services produced by the Databricks healthcare platform. They sit between the foundation and the experiences, making sure dashboards, models, and apps consume consistent, governed data.

Design distinction Certified Metrics, Forecast / Budget, Normalized Views, Interop, and Transaction Detail are not the enabled analytics. They are the services that make the analytics trustworthy, reusable, and scalable.

Certified Metrics

Gold-layer definitions with ownership, lineage, and quality thresholds for executive and operational KPIs.

Enables: Rapid Insights, finance reporting, acquisition performance

Normalized Views

Reusable consumption-ready patient, encounter, provider, claim, order, schedule, and supply views.

Enables: self-service analytics, model inputs, embedded apps

Forecast / Budget

Planning-ready datasets that connect trends, run rates, seasonal factors, and forward-looking assumptions.

Enables: staffing, access, service-line, and financial planning

Interop

FHIR, HL7, API, and partner exchange patterns used to publish or receive operational signals.

Enables: Redox-powered event movement and workflow triggers

Transaction Detail

Traceable record-level data for drill-down across encounters, charges, claims, payments, orders, and operations.

Enables: root cause analysis and reconciliation

Reusable AI Features

Curated features, embeddings, model-ready datasets, and prompt-grounding context governed through the platform.

Enables: predictive models and workflow AI
